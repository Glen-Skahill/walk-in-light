# Solo creator playbook (also inside the app under 📖)

Reviewed against current creator-workflow guidance and classic creative-practice advice. The sources are mostly practitioner blogs; the themes repeat consistently, the statistics in them do not, so treat the numbers as folklore and the practices as tested.

1. **Sequence, do not parallelize.** Launch reviews first (cheapest, fastest feedback, builds the audience), the teaching series second, and keep the story and the music always-on at 15 minutes a day. Three simultaneous launches is how one-person projects die in month four.
2. **Minimum viable brand.** A palette, two fonts, one logo, three templates, one afternoon. You will redo it after ten videos with better taste.
3. **Batch and separate modes.** Writing, shooting and editing use different brains. A writing week, one shoot day, two edit evenings. Mode switching is the hidden burnout cost. Same shirt and light on shoot day so batched videos match.
4. **Buffer of three.** Publish nothing until three episodes are exported, then release weekly or biweekly.
5. **Finish small things** (Zechariah 4:10). A finished short beats an unfinished feature. A bad finished draft beats a perfect outline.
6. **Capture everything, decide weekly.** Inbox in five seconds; triage in the weekly review.
7. **Match the task to the energy.** S 15 min, M 45, L 90+. The "I have…" buttons exist for this.
8. **Repurpose by default.** Every long video yields two Shorts; every review yields a story-bible entry; every script problem yields a lesson idea.
9. **Measure three numbers.** Click-through rate, average view duration, first-30-seconds retention. Not subscribers. Change one thing per video.
10. **Spend nothing, then spend on audio.** Phone, window, free software, free music. First purchase after ten videos: a microphone. For music: headphones first, FL Studio after three finished tracks. Last purchase ever: a camera.
11. **Lore rewards, never gates.** Every video works for a stranger.
12. **Rule of life.** A stop time daily, one day off weekly. A long obedience, not a sprint (Galatians 6:9).
13. **Document the process.** Learning to do this is itself a series.
14. **Back up like it matters.** 3 copies, 2 media, 1 off-site; raw footage off the phone the same day; export the app monthly.

Free toolchain: DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Canva, Audacity, OBS Studio, Obsidian (+ Longform), WriterSolo, YouTube Audio Library. Music: Vital, OTT, TDR Nova, Valhalla Supermassive, Kilohearts Essentials, Waveform Free or LMMS, the FL Studio trial to learn.
