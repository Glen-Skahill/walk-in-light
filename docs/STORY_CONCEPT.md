# Walk in Light — trilogy concept (DRAFT, for you to tear apart)

This is a starting draft so there is something concrete to react to. Every name, rule and beat is replaceable. The same text lives in the app under **Story → Bible → "Trilogy concept, DRAFT"**.

## The one-line theme

**Light is not rationed. It was never scarce. The darkness is a lie we agreed to live under.**

Scriptural spine: John 1:5 · Isaiah 9:2 · Matthew 5:14 · Revelation 22:5.

## The world: Nocturne

A neon megacity under the **Veil**, a canopy of ad-screens, solar collectors and smog raised three generations ago after riots "in the daylight". No one under sixty has seen the sun; officially it is a myth.

The **Helios Authority** sells light through the grid, metered by the lumen. Districts that cannot pay go dark: the **Dimming**. Between the levels run the **Rails**, maintenance tunnels and light-rail conduits that grid technicians use to go anywhere.

Hard rules (never broken):

1. Grid light and true light look different on screen. True light (sun, flame, the Lantern) casts warm shadows, heals slowly, makes screens flicker.
2. The **Lantern** is a pre-Veil archive device holding the Logos file: suppressed Scripture fragments, sky recordings, the last sunrise. It lights only for the one who *carries* it, never the one who *owns* it. It dims when used as a weapon. It cannot be copied; it can be shared.
3. **Glow** is a street drug: synthetic light for the eyes. Feels like daylight, leaves you blinder. The counterfeit (2 Cor 11:14).
4. No one goes up to the Veil. The old service ladders are still on the Rails maps.

Districts: the Crown (Helios HQ, always noon) · Midlight (metered middle class) · the Dimming (candles, barter, the underground church) · the Rails · the Veil itself.

## The cast (max 12 named roles across three films)

| Role | Draft | Want | Need | Lie they believe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | **Mara Quill**, grid technician, 27 | Find her sister lost to the Dimming; keep her light credits paid | To believe light is given, not earned | "You get the light you pay for." |
| Antagonist | **Director Cassian Vale**, Helios | Keep the city ordered and alive | To admit the Veil was built from fear, not love | "People cannot be trusted with the sun." |
| Mentor | **Teodor Abiah**, blind ex-Helios engineer, repair shop on the Rails | See the Lantern carried before he dies | To let the next generation fail | "My hands built the dark; they cannot build the light." |
| Ally | **Jonah Reyes**, Glow dealer going straight | Out of the trade, with money | To stop selling counterfeit light to himself | "Light is a feeling." |

Vale must get one scene per film where the audience almost agrees with him. He saw the daylight riots as a boy; his father died in them. He is right about the riots and wrong about the cure.

## The three films

Each film must satisfy on its own. Write the last image of each film first.

**I. WALK IN LIGHT — light revealed (John 1:5).** Mara finds the Lantern on a repair call in the Dimming. It lights for her before she believes anything (grace precedes faith). The Lamplighters, an underground congregation, have waited sixty years for someone who can carry it where they cannot go: everywhere, on the Rails. She refuses, then carries it. Ending: she lights one dark tower with true light for one night. The city sees it. Teodor is arrested for it. Last image: a tower lit floor by floor, the opposite of the opening image (a tower going dark as its credits run out).

**II. CITY ON A HILL — light shared, at a cost (Matthew 5:14).** The movement grows; districts refuse to pay for light and share it instead. Helios hunts them. Mara is pushed to lead and tempted to win with Helios' tools (force, rationing "for now"). The Lantern dims in her hands when she tries. Jonah betrays them, confesses, is forgiven: the first forgiveness in the trilogy. Teodor dies forgiving. Last image: a city on a hill, lit, and surrounded.

**III. NO MORE NIGHT — light overcoming (Revelation 22:5).** Vale blacks out the entire grid to break them: total night. The Lamplighters climb the Veil by the old ladders. Mara does not conquer; she opens the Veil. Dawn over Nocturne. The sun was always there. Vale faces it: redemption or refusal (decide late; both endings are strong). Last image: the first shadow cast by the sun in sixty years, warm.

## Short stories (minor; also the pilots)

Each short: one theme, one location, two characters, 5 to 8 pages, shootable on a phone.

- *The Candle*: a grid-tech replaces a bulb in a dark district and is offered a candle.
- *Regular*: a Glow dealer's best customer stops buying.
- *Deletion Queue*: a Helios clerk finds a sky photo in the files she is ordered to erase.
- *Rail Song*: two techs argue about a hum in the grid that sounds like singing.
- *Noon*: a day in the Crown, where it is always noon and nobody sleeps well.

## How the other pillars live inside this world

- **Teach** can be *Lamplighter's Workshop*: transmissions from Teodor's repair shop teaching real craft. In Film III his old recordings are what the Lamplighters listen to. The frame can be a 5-second sting or a full persona; the lessons must work for someone who has never heard of Nocturne.
- **Lens** can be an archivist reading "pre-Veil stories" (our anime and films) for the Light hidden in them. Working names: *Through the Veil*, *Signal Watch*, *Light Reading*.
- Rule for both: **lore rewards, never gates.** Every video stands alone.

## Two alternate directions, in case this one is wrong

1. **Pilgrim road** (less cyberpunk-noir, more journey): three films as three legs of a journey out of a dark city toward a rumored dawn; one new companion per film. Easier to shoot on location.
2. **The broadcast**: the protagonist runs a pirate station inside the city; each film is a season of signal vs. jamming. Fits the YouTube-native brand very tightly, and the reviews and lessons become literally in-world broadcasts.
